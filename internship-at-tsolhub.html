ILLUSTRATION AND DESIGN

Brief About Course

This module transforms designers from "asset makers" into "brand architects." We focus on high-stakes visual systems, technical vector precision, and the business of premium branding.

What You Will Learn

  • Vector Engineering: Advanced pathfinding, variable fonts, and "Global Edit" for rapid prototyping.
  • InDesign Power-User: Master pages, GREP styles, and data-merge for high-volume catalogs.
  • Print Architecture: Preparing files for international offset (Spot colors, PDF/X-4 standards).
  • Commercial Retouching: Frequency separation and non-destructive product editing.
Gig Readiness & Professional Presence
  • Finding Gigs: Identifying SMEs in need of brand refreshes and bidding on high-ticket identity projects.
  • Presentation: Crafting "Strategy-First" Behance case studies that show the logic behind the design.
  • Freelance Mastery: Implementing the "Value-Based Pricing" model to move away from hourly rates.

Tools You Will Master

Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, Adobe Photoshop.

Requirements

Basic understanding of design software, a personal portfolio of previous work, and the $80 enrollment fee.

CINEMA, VIDEO & PHOTOGRAPHY

Brief About Course

Focusing on "Cinematic Production," this module covers the physics of light, optics, and the technical rigging required for international film delivery.

What You Will Learn

  • Optics & Light: Mastering the relationship between Native ISO, Shutter Angle, and T-Stops.
  • Lighting Science: Using light meters for High-key and Low-key narrative setups.
  • Cinema Rigging: Hands-on configuration of professional camera bodies (Canon Cinema Line Cameras/Blackmagic).
  • Color Science: DaVinci Resolve workflows and HDR delivery standards.
Gig Readiness & Professional Presence
  • Finding Gigs: Pitching to international agencies as a "Remote Colorist" or high-end B-Roll specialist.
  • Presentation: Creating a Director of Photography (DP) reel that highlights technical consistency.
  • Professionalism: Understanding Call Sheets, production insurance, and agency invoicing.

Tools You Will Master

DaVinci Resolve, Cinema Cameras, XLR Field Audio, Adobe Lightroom.

Requirements

Foundational video knowledge, personal portfolio, and $80 enrollment fee.
